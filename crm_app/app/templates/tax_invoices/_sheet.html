{# The TAX INVOICE A4 sheet - the export invoice restated in INR for the GST / e-way-bill paperwork. Expects `invoice` (an ExportInvoice), `company` and `consignment` (the road-leg lookups assembled in routes/tax_invoices.py). It carries the parent export invoice's OWN number and date: a tax invoice is not separately numbered here. This sheet is deliberately NOT the export invoice's sheet in another currency. It answers a different question - what GST is due on this consignment, and who moved it - so it drops the whole CIF->FOB charges ladder, the shipping routing and the proforma/buyer-order references, and carries instead the road leg (transporter, vehicle, LR), the loading port's PIN and a two-line money block: the goods total and the IGST on it. IGST is the per-product tax the catalog defines, summed, and is NIL under LUT (see ExportInvoice.igst_amount_inr). Every money figure is an own-currency figure converted at `invoice.exchange_rate` (see ExportInvoice.in_inr), printed with Indian digit grouping. Layout is banded: each band is its own pulled up by -1px so its top border collapses onto the band above, letting each band pick its own column widths while the outer frame reads as one continuous grid. The items table and the totals block share one so the Packing / Qty / Alt. Qty totals sit exactly under their columns. Quantity columns map as: Packing = pallets, Qty = boxes, Alt.
