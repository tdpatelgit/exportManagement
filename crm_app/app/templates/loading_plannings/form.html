Loading traces each selected PI through its purchase orders, and pulls in the goods lines actually bought on those orders (priced at the PI's own USD rate). Lines come in at design level, taken from each purchase order's own packing list — a PO orders 1268 boxes of a product, and only its packing list knows those are four designs of 317. A purchase order with no packing list yet comes through at product level instead. Only the goods lines are replaced — the booking, containers and any packing you've already built are kept.

{# ---- Packing: cartons and pallets, built by hand ------------------------ The reason this document exists. `packing_list_items.pallets` has always been stored as boxes/box_per_pallet, a DECIMAL - and 9.91 pallets or 1.5 cartons is not a thing anyone can ship. Here they are real numbered objects that hold any mix of designs and products, so 317 boxes at 32/pallet is nine full pallets plus one holding 29, and 45 + 45 PCS at 30/CTN can be two full cartons plus one mixed 15+15. The carton level is optional: tiles sit straight on the pallet, hardware goes through cartons first. One weight rule covers both - pallet gross = contents net + carton tare + pallet tare. #}

Packing — submit qty for pallet

Auto-build fills whole units to capacity and leaves one part-filled unit per goods line, flagged in orange — it deliberately does not merge them, because that is the judgement call this card exists for. A carton or pallet can hold any mix of designs and products. Capacity and tare come from the product's packing types and can be overtyped on any unit.

Container packing — submit for container

Pallets are assigned whole. VGM is every pallet's gross plus the container's own tare; it turns red past the max permitted weight, which is a warning only — the plan still saves.
