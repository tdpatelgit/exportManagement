Loading traces each ticked purchase order to the batches it was actually produced in — the rows on that order's Production Status page. One line per batch, because a batch number and manufacturing date exist nowhere else, and a pallet is packed out of one firing: ATLANTA LIGHT GREY arrives as two lines, 200 boxes under batch 102 and 117 under 103.
