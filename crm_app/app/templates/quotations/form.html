Choosing a catalog product prefills its name, HSN code, unit and pallet type options. Qty auto-fills from Boxes × the product's Alternate Quantity when known, but stays editable. Pallet type starts on "Loose" — no pallets; picking one of the product's pallet types works out Plts from Boxes using that pallet's box capacity (the dropdown shows how much each pallet stores), and "Manual — Packing" lets Plts be typed by hand, with a Unit dropdown (PLT, CTN, BOX, BAG, PCS, SET) saying which unit that packing is. Price is typed in per line (a specific design and its price are picked later, on the packing list) and is always the absolute FOB price - quantity x price summed across every line is the FOB invoice total, the same no matter which shipping terms are chosen below.

{# Each charge is hidden (and forced to 0) by the delivery terms that don't charge it - see applyDeliveryTermCharges below. #}

FOB value = quantity x price, summed across every line above - the same no matter which shipping terms are chosen. Under FOB terms, CIF/CFR is not calculated or shown at all; invoice value = FOB value + certification + other charges - discount. Under CIF/CFR terms, the CIF/CFR value (FOB total + sea freight + insurance + certification + other charges) is shown too, and invoice value = CIF/CFR value - discount.
